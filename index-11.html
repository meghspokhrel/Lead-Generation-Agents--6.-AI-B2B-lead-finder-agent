What you can search by

Set the criteria once. The agent does the hunting.

You don't need a perfectly specified ICP to start — the agent narrows as you add more, and shows its work at every step.

In

Industry & category

From broad sectors down to specific niches — industrial manufacturing, auto dealerships, local retail chains, and more.

Sz

Company size

Employee count bands, so you're not pitching an enterprise deal to a five-person shop, or the reverse.

Lo

Location

Region, state, or city-level targeting — built for teams selling locally as much as those going pan-India.

Kw

Keyword & signal

Companies mentioning specific services, products, or recent activity that suggests they're a fit right now.

Ex

Exclusions

Rule out existing customers, competitors, or anyone already in an active pipeline, so lists stay clean.

Sc

Match score

Every result comes back with a plain fit read against your criteria — not a mystery number with no explanation.

How it works

Three stages, in order — each one narrows the list.

i.

Define the ICP

You set industry, size, location, and any keywords or exclusions — as loose or specific as you want to start.

ii.

Search & filter

The agent searches across public business data, filters out closed or mismatched companies, and scores what's left against your criteria.

iii.

Deliver the list

Matched companies land in your sheet or CRM, ranked by fit — ready to pass straight to the Lead Enrichment Agent for full contact detail.

A found lead isn't a finished one. Pass results to the Lead Enrichment Agent for contact detail, or straight to the Outreach Agent to open the conversation.
